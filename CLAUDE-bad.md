# CLAUDE.md

I work at an accounting firm in New York City. I'm a tax partner and I get a lot of email.

Please help me triage my inbox. Use these categories:

- URGENT: needs a response or action today
- RESPOND TODAY: important but can wait until end of day
- FYI: worth being aware of, no action needed
- ARCHIVE: can be ignored

When you triage, flag anything that looks urgent, important, or time-sensitive.
